Privacy policy

Sentinel works with public blockchain data and keeps very little about the people who use it. This page lists exactly what the hosted service stores and why.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, names, emails or passwords
  • No private keys or seed phrases, ever
  • No cookies, no analytics or tracking scripts, nothing kept in your browser

The hosting provider also keeps short-lived request logs, which include IP addresses, for operating and debugging the service.

Services that receive data

To answer a request Sentinel sends token addresses, wallet addresses and transactions to:

  • Helius, the Solana RPC provider, for on-chain data and simulations
  • Jupiter, for quotes, swap routes and prices
  • DexScreener, for market data
  • Telegram, for bot messages you opted into
  • The token's own metadata links, for example IPFS gateways

The service runs on Vercel and stores data in a Neon Postgres database. The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

Blockchain data is public

Everything Sentinel analyses is already public on Solana. Transactions you sign and send become public and permanent on-chain, independently of Sentinel.

Your choices

  • Send /stop to the bot to unlink your chat.
  • Run Sentinel yourself from the open-source code if you do not want to use the hosted service at all.
  • To ask about or remove data tied to your wallet, open an issue on GitHub.

Changes

If what we store changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top is updated.
